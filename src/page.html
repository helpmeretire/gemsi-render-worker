What the grades mean

JUNK

A serious trap was found, or so many problems that buying is a gamble you will probably lose.

RISKY

Several real warning signs. Insiders may control the supply, the price or your ability to sell.

CAUTION

Nothing fatal, but something to understand first, such as thin trading or a very new pool.

CLEAN

No major red flags in the contract or market data. That is not the same as a good investment.

What we check

  • Honeypots. Can you actually sell after you buy?
  • Taxes. Hidden buy and sell fees, and whether the owner can raise them.
  • Printing. Whether someone can mint more coins and water yours down.
  • Owner powers. Pausing trades, blacklisting wallets, changing balances.
  • Solana authorities. Live mint and freeze authority on the token.
  • Who holds it. How much of the supply sits in the top 10 wallets.
  • Liquidity. How much real money is in the trading pools.
  • Age. How long it has traded. Most rugs happen in the first days.
__PARTNERS__

This is an automated screen, not advice. It reads public contract and market data and can be wrong, late, or fooled by a clever contract. A CLEAN grade does not mean a token will go up. Never put in money you can't afford to lose.
