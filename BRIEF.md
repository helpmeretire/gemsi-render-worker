# AgingInPlaceGrants.com — shared research brief

Site purpose: help older adults and their adult children find money and plain facts for making a home safe to age in
(grab bars, walk-in showers, ramps, stair lifts, widened doors, etc.). Audience: adults 60+ and their adult kids.
Goal: thick, original, sourced content good enough for Google AdSense approval. No thin pages, no filler, no invented facts.

## Hard rules for all research
- Every fact needs a source URL and the date you checked it (today is 2026-10-18). Prefer official sources (.gov, agency pages, program pages, IRS, VA, USDA, CMS, ACL, state agencies).
- Never invent a number, phone, URL or program. If you can't verify, leave it out or mark it "unverified" — never guess.
- Write all descriptions in your own plain words (8th-grade reading level, short sentences). Never copy wording from a source.
- USC/ACL state funding PDFs are copyrighted ("All Rights Reserved"). Use them ONLY as a lead list of program names, agencies, URLs and phone numbers (facts). Do not copy their description sentences or their eligibility bullet wording.
